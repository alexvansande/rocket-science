Rocket Science

A board game that teaches real rocket physics through interplanetary mission planning. Real engines, real masses, and the rocket equation turned into something you can play at the table.

Card kit Every rocket, equipment and mission card, print-ready (A4, duplex backs). Playtest report Reference missions flown against the board: Apollo 11, Shuttle, Starship to Mars and more. Board The Earth-to-Mars board. Rules (v0 draft) The turn loop: Research, Build, Launch, Move.
